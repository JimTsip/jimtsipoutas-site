Case 05 · PadlBoard

Idea to App Store, in less than 1 month

A padel scoring app taken from idea to the App Store in under a month — designed and built end to end by directing AI agents.

Role
Solo, with AI agents
When
Jul — Aug 2026
Where
iOS · watchOS · Web

Challenge

Every padel scoring app assumes you will put the racket down. Then, mid-build, the database under the live app froze and took the product down with it.

Approach

One interaction — tap the team that won — on watch, phone and web, and a calm rebuild of everything underneath while the product stayed up.

Result

Live on the App Store in Greek and English, with the rebuild shipped as an ordinary update customers never had to notice.

What it is

The short version

Padel is scored by people holding a racket. Every app I tried assumed you would stop playing, look at a phone and tell it who won. PadlBoard is one tap — games, sets, tiebreaks and history all follow from it.

It is three surfaces on one product: the watch on court, the phone for the match, the web for everything after. The hard part was not the app. It was replacing everything underneath it while people were using it.

Process

How it was built

  1. 01

    Cut the work into steps that can be checked

    Every piece went to an agent as something small enough to verify before the next one started. The discipline is not the prompting; it is refusing to move on until a step is proven.

  2. 02

    Design the interaction, then the product

    An eyes-free version tested worse than it read on paper and was dropped. Tap the team that won survived because it needs no instruction.

  3. 03

    One product, three surfaces

    The watch, the phone and the web are views on the same match, not three apps kept in sync with each other.

  4. 04

    Rebuild without taking the product down

    Everything underneath was replaced in stages, and nothing switched over until the checks passed on a clean copy — which is why the change was invisible to the people using it.

Outcome

Where it landed

Live on the App Store in Greek and English. The rebuild went out as version 1.1 and was deliberately left out of the release notes, because it was never a customer feature.

Lesson

What it cost me to learn

Agents are not faster because they type faster. They are faster when the work is cut into steps that can each be proven finished — and slower than doing it by hand when it is not.
